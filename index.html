About

A journey of public life and service.

An overview of B. A. Mohiuddin Bava's background, public service and professional journey.

2013

Entered the Karnataka Legislative Assembly

B. A. Mohiuddin Bava represented the Mangalore City North constituency as a Member of the Legislative Assembly.

B. A. Mohiuddin Bava is a businessman and public figure from Mangaluru, Karnataka. He served as the MLA representing Mangalore City North from 2013 to 2018.

His public profile has included political activity, business interests, sports-related ventures and engagement with the community.

This website is designed as a central place to present his biography, public journey, photographs, activities and contact information.
